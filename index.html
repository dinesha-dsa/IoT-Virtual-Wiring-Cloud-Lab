ESP32 design rules used

  • GPIO34 is input-only and suitable for analog sensing.
  • ESP32 GPIO operates at 3.3 V and is not 5 V tolerant.
  • Motors and pumps require a relay/MOSFET driver and separate supply.
  • All low-voltage control circuits require a valid common reference ground.
